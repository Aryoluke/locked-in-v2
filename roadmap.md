# LOCKED IN roadmap

This file separates the working local foundation from the larger product vision. The following are **stubs/planned integrations**, not claims that they are complete.

## Next product slices

1. Replace the preference-backed store with Drift/SQLite migrations and repository interfaces.
2. Add a self-hosted FastAPI/Node REST service, invite-only auth, encrypted media, backups and background sync with per-field last-write-wins.
3. Add tests, accessibility pass, data export/deletion and privacy visibility controls.

## Explicit roadmap stubs

- **AI coach:** local Ollama/LM Studio first, then free-tier fallback providers, RAG, evidence citations and data-aware plan generation.
- **Form check / body scan:** MediaPipe pose analysis, bad-rep exclusion, body-photo timeline and on-device encryption.
- **Sync:** server source of truth, offline queue, retries, conflict UI and cross-device account sync.
- **Squad chat/feed:** invite approvals, private chat, reactions, brag cards and presence.
- **Arcs:** winter/exam/summer arcs, check-ins, arc XP, badges and end reports.
- **Competitions:** weekly/monthly challenges, duels, raids, sport scoring, battle royale and rankings.
- **Skill drills:** muscle-up/front lever/planche paths, speaking, voice analysis, memory, maths, style and cold-exposure drills.
- **Food plans:** dietary-safe meal planner, what's-at-home mode, recipes, macros/micros, barcode/photo logging, grocery/cost tracking.
- **Skin system:** profile, routines, acne/scar/dermatitis logs, product and ingredient library, SPF and weekly reports.
- **Multi-sport:** first-class rugby, football, tennis, swimming, cricket, hockey, running, cycling, boxing, climbing and other activity schemas, drills and leaderboards.
- **Wearables:** Amazfit/Health Connect, sleep/HR/HRV and recovery imports.
- **Recovery/growth:** sleep debt, fatigue map, mobility, injury/rehab, age-aware growth education and parent-height estimate.
- **Integrations:** Google Calendar, Strava, music, NFC/QR check-in, weather-aware runs, screen-time APIs and self-hosted push.
- **Premium polish:** motion, haptics, confetti/PR cards, widgets, themes, avatar showcase, shop, records board, friend streaks and weekly wrap.

Roadmap modules should keep privacy-first defaults, transparent permissions, export/delete controls and age-appropriate safety copy. No paid features or real-money currency are planned.
